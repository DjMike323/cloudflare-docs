## Key Insights

**HERMES Trinity excels at:**
- Adaptive intelligence and autonomous decision-making
- Rapid deployment (15 minutes to production)
- Learning from every interaction
- Cost-efficient scaling ($1-5K/month at 1M requests)

**KAI excels at:**
- Deterministic, predictable workflows
- Visual control and team coordination
- Audit trails and manual optimization
- Clear structure for complex pipelines

**Best practice:** Use both systems together. HERMES provides autonomous routing; KAI provides proven workflows. Not competitors—complementary.
